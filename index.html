Time Away

See the time you actually got back

Counts phone-free time strictly during your waking hours (7 AM - 11 PM), so sleep time doesn't inflate the number.

  • Today's phone-free total, updated as you go
  • Current break and longest continuous daytime break
  • A 7-day view of hours reclaimed, with a bar for each day
  • Runs on Android's usage-access permission, granted in one tap
